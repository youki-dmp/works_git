# Cinematic Visual Proposal for 'Kumiko Sato'
**Role: Director of Photography (Cameraman)**

Based on the review of the current assets and the project strategy for 'AI Talent', I propose the following visual techniques to enhance Kumiko Sato's presence.

## 1. Current Asset Review (Observations)
- **Status:** Scene videos (MP4) exist for early scripts.
- **Lighting:** Functional, but standard. Lacks the "premium" warmth associated with high-end instructional content.
- **Framing:** Mostly consistent, but needs more dynamic depth to maintain engagement for 48s+ durations.
- **Vibe:** Intellectual and gentle (as intended), but can be elevated to feel more like a high-production broadcast.

## 2. Visual Enhancement Techniques

### A. Lighting Strategy: 'The Wisdom Glow'
- **Key Light:** Soft, warm light (approx. 3200K-4000K) positioned at 45 degrees to create gentle depth on the face.
- **Rim Light:** A subtle cool-white rim light to separate Kumiko from the background, emphasizing professional polish.
- **Eye Catch:** Ensure a distinct catchlight in the eyes to foster trust and "aliveness."

### B. Framing & Composition: 'The Golden Ratio Mentor'
- **The "Rule of Space":** Give Kumiko more "looking room" in side-profile shots to make the viewer feel like they are in a conversation.
- **Depth of Field:** Use a shallow depth of field (bokeh) for background separation, using textures like a soft-focus library or a sunlit living room to imply "Learned Comfort."
- **Z-Axis Movement:** Subtle slow zooms (0.95x to 1.05x) during key emotional points to draw the viewer in.

### C. Transitions: 'Invisible Flow'
- **Soft J-Cuts/L-Cuts:** Audio should precede the visual change slightly to make transitions feel natural.
- **Light Leaks:** Use very subtle, warm light leak overlays (low opacity) between scenes to create a nostalgic, "Show-era" warmth.

## 3. Cinematic Preset: "Sato Warmth 2026"
To ensure consistency across Nano Banana Pro (Images) and Veo 3 (Videos), I propose the following prompt-level preset:

> **Visual Style:** 8k UHD, cinematic lighting, shot on 35mm lens, f/1.8. 
> **Color Grade:** Warm highlights, slightly desaturated shadows, soft film grain. 
> **Subject Profile:** Kumiko Sato (54yo), intelligent, gentle smile, soft linen clothing, natural silver-streaked hair.
> **Environment:** Blurred 'modern Japanese study' background with soft morning sunlight.

## 4. Coordination with Creative Director
- **Alignment:** The visuals prioritize "Trust" (Trust-driven design) over "Flashiness."
- **Engagement:** We will use "Z-axis scaling" in Remotion to prevent visual stagnation during longer educational segments.
- **Feedback Request:** Does the 'Sato Warmth 2026' preset align with the proposed LINE funnel's "gentle mentor" vibe?
