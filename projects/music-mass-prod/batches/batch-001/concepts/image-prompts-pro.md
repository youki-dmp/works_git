# Image Generation Prompts (High Quality / Pro)

For each track, use these high-detail prompts in "Nano Banana Pro" or your preferred high-end image generator. Aim for 16:9 aspect ratio.

## General Style Guidelines
- **Style**: Lo-fi aesthetic, cinematic lighting, high-detail textures, masterpiece, 8k resolution, atmospheric, nostalgic, emotional.
- **Lighting**: Soft glow, neon reflections, golden hour, or moody moonlight.

---

## 1. Shinjuku Neon Rain
**Prompt**: 
A high-detail cinematic shot of a narrow Shinjuku alleyway at night during a gentle rain. Neon signs in blue and pink reflect in the puddles on the ground. A single vending machine glows softly in a corner. Soft bokeh in the background. Masterpiece, atmospheric lo-fi aesthetic, 8k, highly detailed.

## 2. Kyoto Sunset Zen
**Prompt**: 
A breathtaking view of a traditional Japanese pagoda in Kyoto during a vibrant orange and purple sunset. Cherry blossom petals are slowly falling in the breeze. Soft, warm sunlight filters through the trees. Peaceful, zen, highly detailed cinematic scenery, 8k, emotional atmosphere.

## 3. Midnight Konbini
**Prompt**: 
A lonely but cozy convenience store (Konbini) standing in a quiet urban neighborhood at 3 AM. The bright white and blue light from the store spills onto the dark street. Muted city colors, nostalgic atmosphere, vaporwave-influenced lighting, 8k resolution, highly detailed.

## 4. School Rooftop Breeze
**Prompt**: 
An empty high school rooftop in Japan during the blue hour after school. A chain-link fence overlooks a sprawling suburban sunset. A single forgotten school bag on a bench. The wind is visible through the movement of light curtains in the distance. Anime-inspired hyper-realistic style, 8k, nostalgic, peaceful.

## 5. Tokyo Subway Dreams
**Prompt**: 
Inside a nearly empty Tokyo subway car at night. Cinematic lighting coming from the overhead fluorescent lights, creating long shadows. Outside the window, city lights blur into streaks of light. Introspective, moody, high-detail metal and glass textures, 8k, urban journey.
